Refund Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

This policy covers the $99 Google Business Profile audit sold on tacticalsi.biz by Tactical Si.

Within 24 hours, before delivery: full refund

You can get a full refund if you ask within 24 hours of ordering and before your report has been delivered.

Delivered reports

Once your report has been delivered, it's yours to keep, so delivered reports aren't refundable.

If we're late

We email your report within one business day after we receive your completed intake form. If we miss that one-business-day delivery window, you can ask for a full refund.

If something is wrong with your report

If your report is about the wrong business or is missing a part described in our Terms of Service, tell us within 14 days and we'll fix it. We don't give refunds because a ranking, a number of calls or an AI tool's answer didn't change after the audit.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@tacticalsi.io from the email you ordered with, or include it in your message, along with your business name. We'll confirm by email. Approved refunds go back to the card you paid with through Stripe. Your bank decides how long it takes to show up, usually a few business days.

Charged by mistake?

If you were charged twice or by mistake, email us and we'll fix it.

Monthly plans: cancel any time

The Core ($349/month), Grow ($599/month) and Dominate ($799/month) plans are month to month, priced per Google Business Profile location. There is no long-term contract. You can cancel any time by emailing hello@tacticalsi.io. Your cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid month, your plan keeps running until then, and you won't be charged again after that. There are no refunds for partial months.
