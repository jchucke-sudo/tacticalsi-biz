How to use your report

Fix wrong information first. Incorrect hours, a wrong category or an old address do more damage than a missing post.

Then work the fail list top to bottom. The fix list is in priority order, so start at item one.

Treat the maps and AI results as a snapshot. Rankings move day to day. Compare a later audit with the same keywords to see what changed.

Profile audit vs. full local SEO

This audit focuses on your Google Business Profile, your Maps rankings and AI visibility, which is where most local businesses see results fastest.
